Cookie & Digital Telemetry Policy

Effective Date: January 1, 2026 • Registered Address: 181 Mercer Street, New York, NY 10012

1. Categorization & Functional Use of Cookies

The digital platforms of ALMOND MUNCHY employ minimal, privacy-centric HTTP cookies and local storage tokens to deliver rapid page loading, intuitive mobile navigation, and secure form validation. Cookies are compact text tokens deposited upon your computing device that enable our servers to retain your navigation states, shopping cart records, and mobile drawer configurations across active browsing sessions. These fundamental operational tokens cannot execute autonomous executable binaries, infect your operating system, or access private files stored upon your hard drive.

We classify our digital cookies into strictly necessary functional tokens and aggregated analytical telemetry markers. Necessary functional tokens manage secure communication with our 181 Mercer Street reservation database, protect user inputs against malicious request forgery, and remember chosen checkout options. Analytical telemetry tokens, including Google Analytics Tag G-0LY0HY7L01, gather anonymized performance metrics regarding page loading latency, viewport resolutions, and content readership to guide our engineering team in optimizing platform responsiveness worldwide.

2. Third-Party Telemetry & Analytics Management

All performance telemetry processed through our digital web properties is maintained in anonymized formats that mask individual internet protocol addresses prior to long-term analytical storage. We strictly prohibit external advertising networks from depositing behavioral tracking beacons, third-party marketing pixels, or invasive device fingerprinting scripts across our web domain. The aggregated statistical insights compiled through our analytics tools are utilized solely by our in-house culinary directors and web developers to ensure that our roastery presentations load rapidly across desktop and mobile devices.

Patrons possess complete autonomy to accept, decline, or configure cookie storage preferences through their personal browser settings control panels at any time. Major modern web browsers including Apple Safari, Google Chrome, Mozilla Firefox, and Microsoft Edge provide comprehensive preference interfaces allowing users to clear existing cookies or block future incoming tokens. Please note that disabling essential functional cookies may affect the interactive behavior of our tasting reservation form or require manual re-entry of roastery inquiry details.

3. Policy Updates & Institutional Inquiries

We periodically review our digital cookie governance protocols to ensure full compliance with evolving statutory privacy regulations, browser security mandates, and updated web development standards. Any material modifications to our cookie governance practices will be promptly documented on this page alongside an updated legal revision calendar date. Your continued engagement with our web platform following published modifications signifies your informed comprehension and ongoing agreement to our updated technical cookie governance standards across all sessions.
