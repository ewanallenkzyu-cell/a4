1. Tree Nut Allergen Warnings & Facility Disclosures

ALMOND MUNCHY manufactures and roasts whole tree nut products, primarily sweet California almonds (Prunus dulcis), along with occasional small-batch walnut and hazelnut seasonal releases. Individuals possessing diagnosed medical allergies or acute sensitivities to tree nuts must exercise extreme caution and avoid consuming our products. Our roasting and packaging equipment operates within an active nut-processing environment, and we cannot guarantee that any product dispatched from our 181 Mercer Street facility is free from tree nut allergens.

While our facility strictly excludes peanuts, wheat gluten, soy, dairy, shellfish, and artificial synthetic preservatives from our production floor, atmospheric airborne nut proteins are inherently present throughout our processing rooms. Patrons with severe anaphylactic histories should consult their board-certified allergist before visiting our SoHo tasting room or sampling our warm roasted batches. We clearly label all packaging tins with prominent allergen declarations in full compliance with United States Food and Drug Administration labeling statutes.

2. Natural Agricultural Variations & Shell Fragments

Almonds are natural agricultural tree crops harvested from living orchards subjected to variable weather patterns, seasonal sunshine, and organic soil conditions. While our state-of-the-art optical sorters and hand-inspection tables eliminate foreign material with greater than ninety-nine percent efficiency, patrons should be mindful that minute shell fragments or natural kernel blemishes can occasionally occur. Such occurrences are typical of authentic minimally processed agricultural foods and do not signify defective manufacturing procedures or substandard product quality.

The nutritional treatises and culinary descriptions published on our website are provided for informational and educational purposes only. Statements regarding the cardiovascular benefits of monounsaturated fats or antioxidant properties of almond skins have not been evaluated by the FDA to diagnose, treat, cure, or prevent any clinical disease. Patrons seeking therapeutic dietary modifications should seek individualized counsel from licensed medical practitioners before making significant alterations to their nutritional regimens.

3. Shelf Stability & Administration Contact

Our artisan dry-roasted almonds are crafted without artificial chemical preservatives, synthetic emulsifiers, or hydrogenated stabilizing oils. To preserve their signature crispy texture and delicate aromatic bouquet, sealed tins should be stored in a dry, cool environment away from direct radiant heat, steam, or high humidity. We accept zero liability for product degradation, moisture softening, or flavor loss resulting from improper storage conditions, prolonged heat exposure, or delayed refrigeration following container opening.
