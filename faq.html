Frequently Asked Questions

Technical details regarding our small-batch roasting protocols, allergen safety, shelf stability, and salon appointments in SoHo, New York.

Commercial oil-roasting involves submerging nuts in vats of heated palm, canola, or cottonseed oil. This adds rancidity-prone inflammatory fats and destroys natural antioxidants. ALMOND MUNCHY uses only circulating hot air at 138 degrees Celsius, allowing the almond's own natural oleic oils to crisp the kernel from within without any added oils.

Our hermetically sealed nitrogen-flushed tins maintain peak oven-fresh crispness for twelve months unopened. Once opened, we recommend consuming within thirty days or storing in a cool, dark pantry away from direct heat to preserve delicate aromatic nuances.

Our 181 Mercer Street facility processes tree nuts (specifically almonds, walnuts, and hazelnuts). We do NOT process peanuts, gluten, soy, dairy, or artificial preservatives on our dedicated nut roasting equipment. Every batch undergoes strict allergen hygiene sanitation between shifts.

Our flagship roastery and tasting salon is situated at 181 Mercer Street, New York, NY 10012, United States. Guided forty-minute tasting flights are hosted Monday through Saturday by advance appointment. Reservations can be made online or by telephone at +1-888-777-5845.
