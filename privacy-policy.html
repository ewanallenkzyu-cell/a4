Privacy Policy & Patron Data Governance

Effective Date: January 1, 2026 • Registered Address: 181 Mercer Street, New York, NY 10012

1. Roastery Information Gathering Protocols

Patrons engaging ALMOND MUNCHY through our tasting reservation portal or wholesale roastery services submit personal credentials including full names, physical postal addresses, electronic mail information, and customized dietary specifications. These discrete data assets are compiled solely to fulfill small-batch almond orders, coordinate private tasting appointments, and ensure flawless white-glove product dispatch. We handle patron information with strict confidentiality, maintaining institutional security safeguards appropriate for an artisanal culinary purveyor operating within the State of New York.

Our web infrastructure automatically captures standardized telemetry records including terminal browser identifiers, operating systems, session timestamps, and network routing addresses. This technical telemetry is evaluated in aggregated non-personalized formats to optimize server throughput, prevent unauthorized automated script traffic, and verify that reservation forms transmit securely to our dispatch desk at 181 Mercer Street. We audit our digital firewall rules periodically to guarantee that patron transactional records remain thoroughly protected against unauthorized external intrusion.

2. Data Processing Architecture & Storage

Personal credentials gathered during roastery purchases, tasting bookings, and corporate gift arrangements are maintained on hardened encrypted databases featuring role-based credential permissions. Only authorized roastery managers, concierge dispatchers, and financial compliance officers possess access privileges to review client transaction records or delivery notes. We enforce comprehensive confidentiality agreements with all staff members to protect your personal identity, order frequencies, and customized seasoning preferences throughout the entire lifespan of our culinary engagement.

We do not market, monetize, rent, or otherwise disseminate client contact records to third-party marketing brokers, commercial data vendors, or external behavioral advertising syndicates under any circumstances. External disclosures are restricted strictly to verified payment gateway clearinghouses and bonded freight delivery services responsible for transporting sealed roastery tins directly to your residence. Every external logistics partner contractually pledges full adherence to United States federal privacy statutes and our rigorous institutional security protocols.

3. Patron Rights & Administrative Contact

Patrons possess full statutory entitlements under applicable state, federal, and international privacy laws to review, update, or permanently expunge their personal records from our roastery databases. You may request a verified accounting of all identifiable personal data retained in our active storage systems by submitting a formal inquiry to our administration office. We process authenticated data disclosure and deletion petitions within thirty business days, ensuring complete adherence to statutory commercial guidelines.

If you have questions regarding our data privacy procedures, wish to amend your salon communication preferences, or require formal legal documentation, please contact our administrative team directly at 181 Mercer Street, New York, NY 10012, United States. Direct telephone inquiries may also be placed to our client service line at +1-888-777-5845 during regular business hours. We remain resolutely committed to ensuring that your private credentials receive the same uncompromised care and integrity that distinguishes our artisanal almond roasts.
